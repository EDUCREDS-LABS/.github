### What We Build

EduCreds is **credential trust infrastructure** that helps education institutions issue verifiable credentials and gives employers and verifiers a dependable way to validate authenticity and credential status.

We connect institutional identity, credential issuance, cryptographic proof, and verification into one coherent trust experience — without exposing private student data.

### How It Works

```
Institution → Issues credential → Signed & anchored on-chain → Student holds & shares → Verifier checks in seconds
```

**For Institutions:** Issue tamper-evident credentials with institutional authority. Bulk issuance, lifecycle management, governance controls.

**For Students & Holders:** Own your credentials. Share them anywhere with privacy controls. No dependency on office hours.

**For Employers & Verifiers:** Verify a credential's authenticity and status in seconds — without phone calls, PDFs, or manual back-and-forth.

### Technical Foundation

- 🔐 **W3C Verifiable Credentials** — Standards-based, interoperable, portable
- 🆔 **Decentralized Identifiers (DIDs)** — Institutional and student identity primitives
- ⛓️ **Multi-chain anchoring** — Gasless issuance, on-chain proof of integrity
- 🛡️ **Proof of Institutional Credibility (PoIC)** — Trust scoring for issuing institutions
- ✅ **Verification Intelligence** — AI-powered document and domain verification
